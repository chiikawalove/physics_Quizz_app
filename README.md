Physics Quiz

A Swift-based physics learning app that explores the physics behind everyday phenomena.

About

Physics is often perceived as difficult or abstract, even though many physical phenomena can be found in everyday life.

This project aims to help students discover the physics behind familiar things through quizzes and short explanations.
The quiz content and explanations are designed based on my knowledge as an awardee of the Physics Challenge Japan.Also, my physics teacher checked these answers,too.

Features

* Physics quizzes organized by topic and difficulty
* Questions based on everyday phenomena
* Explanations for each question
* Level completion tracking
* “Daily Physics” feature that introduces one physics phenomenon each day
* Images to make physical phenomena easier to visualize

Topics

The app currently covers topics such as:

* Mechanics
* Electricity
* Waves
* Heat
* Atomic physics
* Interdisciplinary physics

Development

This app is being developed with Swift and SwiftUI.Developed with Swift and SwiftUI. AI was utilized to help refine the text length and ensure the completeness of the explanations.
